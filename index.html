Spectrophotometer

Step 1

Placement of Cuvette


Fill the cuvette with a testable solution and place the cuvette in its designated holder.

Step 2

Color Selection


Select the color of the sample using the dropdown menu.

Step 3

Scan Color Position


The linear rail base will scan the wavelengths of visible light using a color sensor and align itself with the selected color's trajectory to allow the corresponding wavelength of light to enter the slit.

Step 4

Manually move sensor


The color sensor (located in between the slit and cuvette) must be slid off to the side manually for the beam of light to pass through the sample.
